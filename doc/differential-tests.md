# Differential tests (zSide, slot 98)

Their code runs **locally only** (user approval, 2026-10-22): iwakura-rein/thunder-orchard
b225827 built from `~/skydoge/ext/thunder-orchard` (`thunder_orchard_app`,
`thunder_orchard_app_cli`), next to ecash-com/bitcoin (betanet branch) regtest, electrs and
bip300301_enforcer 17eb2ce (`~/skydoge/ext/PINNED.txt`). Never on servers.

## A. Byte level (no network)

1. `tests/data/betanet/blocks.bin`: all public betanet blocks, fetched over QUIC from
   `seed.beta.ecash.eu.com:4098` (`zside-fetch-blocks`). `bt_tests` decodes each, re-encodes
   it byte for byte, checks every hash against the seed index and replays it with every check
   (BMM against the public enforcer's GetTwoWayPegData for slot 98, utreexo proofs and roots,
   FROST signatures, Orchard anchors, binding/spend-auth signatures and halo2 proofs through
   the reference crate, nullifiers, frontier), then rolls the whole history back to genesis
   and reconnects it.
2. Oracle on THEIR types crate (`thunder_orchard_types`, MIT): a 40-line Rust program
   (scratchpad `zs/oracle`, depends on `ext/thunder-orchard/types` by path) decodes the same
   `blocks.bin` with their serde/bincode, re-encodes it, and prints per block the block hash,
   `Body::compute_merkle_root`, every `txid()` and `borsh` size, and the Orchard frontier root
   after appending every cmx. `ZS_DUMP=<file> build/tests/bt_tests` writes the same lines from
   our code; the two files must be identical.
3. `tests/test_zside_orchard.cpp`: EmptyAnchor, nullifier double spend refused only at
   connect, txid ignores proofs/signatures, memo-overlap layout, decode refusals (proof
   size, unknown flag bits, identity rk and epk, non-canonical nullifier).

## B. Regtest, side by side (`tests/regtest/zside_regtest.py full`)

ecashd + electrs + the enforcer, slot 98 proposed and activated through the enforcer's block
producer; THEIR node and OURS peered only with each other; each `mine` RPC (BMM request
through the enforcer, the mainchain block generated by the enforcer) alternates between the
two. After every block: `getblockcount`, best side/main hashes, `list_utxos` (as sets),
pending bundle, latest failed bundle, sidechain wealth, `get_block` (both verbosities),
`get_block_hash`, `get_bmm_inclusions`, `get_utxos`/`get_stxos` of both wallets — raw
JSON-RPC reply bytes compared. At the end the whole history is compared again.

Steps: empty blocks; deposits to both (bare base58 address); transparent transfers both
ways; shields on both; shielded transfers both ways (THEIR wallet's bundle spent by OUR
wallet's notes and vice versa); unshields; withdrawals until the bundle is Submitted and
Confirmed (paid on the mainchain); our shielded tx relayed to theirs and mined by theirs,
theirs to ours and mined by ours; a mainchain reorg over the two last side blocks (see
zside.md); restarts; fresh nodes of each implementation syncing
only from the other; our node with their mnemonic derives their transparent and shielded
addresses; `openapi_schema`.
